Sprinkle Shooter
fires a sprinkle every x seconds that travels right along its row and damages the first ant it hits. The sprinkle is small and fast.

Sugar Cannon
fires less often but hits harder. It could also knock ants back a little.

Cinnamon Blaster
fires a short-range puff that hits every ant within about two tiles. It suits crowded rows.

Cookie Crumbler
fires crumbs that stop after a short distance and deal damage over time. Good for slowing ants without a big hit.

Honey Trap
doesn’t attack. Ants that walk into it are slowed for a few seconds. It works well alongside a shooter.

Oven Toaster
fires a flame that burns every ant in its row for a few seconds. It’s stronger but costs more flour.

Frosting Piper
fires a frosting blob that sticks an ant in place for a short time, so it can’t move while it takes damage.
